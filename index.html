Privacy

Your anonymous feedback is always secure. You can manage data sharing preferences here.

How Anonymity Works

Your feedback is submitted through a secure process that separates your identity from the feedback text.

Reviewers can see:

  • Feedback category
  • Selected area
  • Rating
  • Feedback text
  • Suggestion
  • Severity
  • Submission date
  • Anonymous Feedback ID

Reviewers cannot see:

  • Student name
  • Student ID
  • Email
  • Profile photo
  • Personal account info
